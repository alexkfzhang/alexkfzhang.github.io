Kaifeng Zhang

I am a Research Scientist at Sharpa Robotics. I have previously worked for NetEase AI Lab, Tencent Robotics X, Huawei (Autonomous Driving) and Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute.

My work lies in machine learning, computer vision and robotics. In particular, my research focuses on solving (1) high cost for training data in robotic domain, (2) high cost for policy learning in the real-world, and (3) high cost for model evaluation in the real-world.
